🎃 A few things on in Porto this weekend (30 Oct – 1 Nov)

🎻 Schubert & Strauss: the Porto Symphony Orchestra with Lucas Macías as oboe soloist and conductor (Strauss's Oboe Concerto, Schubert's "Unfinished"). Fri 30 Oct, 21:00, Casa da Música (Sala Suggia). €26–30.

🎭 Free in Matosinhos: "O mar em todos os cantos da casa" (part of FITEI), theatre, music and visual art built from a local community's stories. Thu 29 & Fri 30 Oct, 21:30, Antiga Fábrica Vasco da Gama. Free, book at reservas@fitei.com.

🏠 Free talk at Serralves: "How will we live in 2074?" with SAGG Napoli and Portuguese architects, on the future of housing. Sat 31 Oct, 15:00, Serralves Contemporary Gallery. Free.

🎸 Local live music for Halloween night: HETTA + MANGUALDE + TAPE at Radioclube Agramonte. Sat 31 Oct, 22:00. €10 in advance (€12 at the door).

💃 Halloween with salsa and afro beats: two dance floors (AfroMuxima + Latin Planet) at Muxima Bar, costumes encouraged. Sat 31 Oct, 23:00–04:00. €10.

🕯️ For All Saints' Day: "De Profundis", the Casa da Música Choir sings Pärt, Mendelssohn and Pinho Vargas. Sun 1 Nov, 18:00, Sala Suggia. €22.
