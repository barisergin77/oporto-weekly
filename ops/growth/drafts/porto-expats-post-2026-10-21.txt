🗓️ A few things on in Porto this weekend (23–25 Oct)

🎸 Daniel Casares Sextet with Cuca Roseta: Spanish flamenco guitar meets one of fado's big voices. Sat 24 Oct, 21:00, Casa da Música (Sala Suggia). Tickets €20–35.

🎤 Marina Sena, "Coisas Naturais": Brazilian pop (samba, reggae, axé vibes). Sat 24 Oct, 21:30, Coliseu Porto Ageas. From €35.

🖼️ Alice Neel: Beautifully Imperfect at Serralves: the American portrait painter's big exhibition, on until January. Museum ticket €20. Pair it with a walk in the Serralves park while the autumn colours are out.

👶 Free for families: the Nano Mini Micro festival at Biblioteca Municipal Almeida Garrett (next to Palácio de Cristal) runs free shows and workshops for babies and kids through December. Worth checking this Saturday's session.
