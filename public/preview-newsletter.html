Issue 008 · Your Weekly Porto Guide

Spring in the City

April 16 – 22, 2026
From the Editor

Porto shrugs off the last chill this week. Six Bridges Fest takes over the Douro, the Serralves gardens are finally in full bloom, and the first rooftop season of the year kicks off at Pestana Vintage. Below, the week's must-dos.

✦

Editor's Picks

THE TOP FIVE
1

Six Bridges Jazz Festival

Fri 18 Apr, 20:00·Casa da Música·€25

Four evenings of Portuguese and international jazz under the Douro's bridges. Highlights: Maria João's closing set, Salvador Sobral guest appearance, and a free riverside stage in Vila Nova de Gaia.

Serralves em Festa

Sat–Sun, all day·Serralves Gardens·Free

Tip of the Week

Ribeira at sunrise

Skip the queues and head to the riverside at 7am. The bakery on Rua da Reboleira opens at 7:30 with the best broa de milho in town. Eat it on the quay, watch the rabelo boats wake up. Free, magical, not on TripAdvisor.
