Baris here, editor of Oporto Weekly. Porto works well with children: there is an aquarium by the sea, a museum with a boat ride, old trams, a zoo and a wildlife park just over the river, and big free parks for when everyone needs to run around. This guide lists the places worth your time, with the prices and hours I could confirm. Where I couldn't confirm them, I've linked the official site.

Attractions Kids Enjoy in Porto

SEA LIFE Porto

The aquarium sits on the seafront next to Castelo do Queijo, at the edge of Parque da Cidade. The walk-through ocean tunnel is the part children remember. It is a good plan for a rainy day.

  • Where: 1ª Rua Particular do Castelo do Queijo, 4100-379 Porto
  • When: open daily
  • Prices: basic tickets from €19 per adult; an annual pass is €44. Book on visitsealife.com/porto

World of Discoveries

An interactive museum about the Portuguese voyages of discovery, with a ship interior to explore and a short boat ride through the exhibits. It suits primary-school ages best.

  • Where: Rua de Miragaia 106, 4050-387 Porto
  • When: Tuesday to Friday 10:00 to 18:00 (last entry 17:30); weekends and public holidays 10:00 to 19:00 (last entry 18:30); closed Mondays, 25 December and 1 January
  • Prices: check worldofdiscoveries.com

Museu do Carro Eléctrico (Tram Museum)

Old trams in the former Massarelos power station, on the riverside tram line 1. Children can see the cars up close, and it pairs well with a ride on the historic tram.

  • Where: Alameda Basílio Teles 51, Porto
  • When: Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00 (last entry 30 minutes before closing)
  • Prices: adults €8, ages 6 to 18 €4, under 6 free; family ticket (1 adult + 1 child) €8, plus €5 per extra adult and €3 per extra child. Discounts with some tram and Andante tickets, see museudocarroelectrico.pt

3D Fun Art Museum

Rooms of optical-illusion paintings that you step into for photos. Silly, hands-on, and good for an hour.

  • Where: Rua de Faria Guimarães 712, 4200-289 Porto
  • When: daily, 10:00 to 19:00

The Chocolate Experience at WOW

In the WOW cultural district in Vila Nova de Gaia, this chocolate museum goes from cacao to a working factory, and the visit includes tastings. Tickets on wow.pt.

  • Where: WOW, Rua do Choupelo 39, Vila Nova de Gaia

FC Porto Museum and Stadium Tour

For football fans. The combined museum and stadium tour at Estádio do Dragão takes you down the players' tunnel to pitch level.

  • Where: Estádio do Dragão, Via Futebol Clube do Porto, 4350-415 Porto
  • Prices: museum + stadium tour €25 for adults (13 to 64), €13 for children 5 to 12, €20 for over-65s; under 5 free. Tickets on store.fcporto.pt

Casa da Música Guided Tours

The concert hall's building tour is a good one for older children who like architecture. The hall also has a family programme of concerts and workshops; check casadamusica.com.

  • Where: Avenida da Boavista 604-610, 4149-071 Porto
  • Tour prices: €12; ages 13 to 18 €5; under 12 free with a paying adult. Which rooms you see depends on that day's programme

Zoo Santo Inácio

A large zoo in Avintes, on the Gaia side. Lions and lemurs are among the residents, and the lion feeding is the moment to aim for. You'll want a car or a taxi.

  • Where: Rua 5 de Outubro 4503, 4430-809 Avintes, Vila Nova de Gaia
  • When: daily; 10:00 to 16:30 in winter, later in the warmer months
  • Prices: adults €18.90, children €12.90, seniors €16.90, family ticket €60.90. Tickets on zoosantoinacio.com

Parque Biológico de Gaia

Also in Avintes, a nature park with a walking trail through woods and fields and native Iberian animals. It is very cheap.

  • Where: Rua Cunha, 4430-812 Avintes, Vila Nova de Gaia
  • Prices: adults €3, ages 7 to 17 €1, under 6 free, family ticket (up to 6 people) €7.50. See parquebiologico.pt

On the Water and in the Air

Several operators run "six bridges" cruises on the Douro from the Ribeira and Gaia quays. It is an easy outing for small children; book ahead in summer.

The Teleférico de Gaia cable car runs between the Jardim do Morro (at the Gaia end of the Dom Luís I Bridge's upper deck) and the Cais de Gaia riverfront. Walking across the bridge and riding the cable car down saves little legs a very steep hill.

The historic tram line 1 runs along the river between Infante and Passeio Alegre in Foz, past the Tram Museum. It is run by STCP and you buy the ticket on board. Andante Tour cards are not valid on it.

Surf Lessons in Matosinhos

For teenagers who want to try surfing, Matosinhos beach (metro line A to Matosinhos Sul) has a row of surf schools along the seafront. Our beach guide lists the ones I checked. Ask each school about minimum ages and prices.

Parks for Families

Parque da Cidade

Porto's biggest park, with lakes, lawns for picnics, playgrounds and paths that take you out to the seafront, close to SEA LIFE. Free.

  • Where: entrances on Avenida da Boavista and Estrada Interior da Circunvalação, Porto

Jardins do Palácio de Cristal

Gardens with views over the Douro, lawns and the peacocks that wander freely, which most children find more exciting than the view. Free.

  • Where: Rua de Dom Manuel II, 4050-346 Porto
  • When: 8:00 to 19:00 in winter, later in summer

Parque de Serralves

The park around the contemporary art museum has formal gardens, woodland and the Treetop Walk, a raised walkway through the tree canopy.

  • Where: Rua D. João de Castro 210, 4150-417 Porto
  • Prices: park only €15; all spaces €24; children under 12 free; ages 12 to 17 half price. Family ticket (2 full + 2 discounted) €58. Residents of Portugal pay less at the ticket desk and get in free on the first Sunday of the month. See serralves.pt

Jardim Botânico and the Galeria da Biodiversidade

The University of Porto's botanical garden has cactus gardens, greenhouses and a lake. Inside it, the Galeria da Biodiversidade is a hands-on science museum about the diversity of life, a good indoor stop.

  • Where: Rua do Campo Alegre 1191, Porto
  • Galeria prices: €5; ages 5 to 17 €2.50; under 4 free; family ticket (2 adults and children up to 17) €14. Free on the second Sunday of the month, 10:00 to 13:00. Check mhnc.up.pt for hours

Parque do Covelo

A hillside park in the east of the city with a big playground. Free.

  • Where: Rua de Bolama, 4200-042 Porto
  • When: daily, 7:00 to 20:00

Small Museums Worth a Stop

Porto Legends

Porto's myths and legends told with 360º projections and sound effects, in the old tunnels under the Alfândega (customs house).

  • Where: Furnas da Alfândega do Porto, Rua Nova da Alfândega 59, 4050-430 Porto
  • When: closed Mondays; Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 to 13:00 and 16:30 to 18:00

Museu das Marionetas do Porto

The puppet museum of the Teatro de Marionetas do Porto, with puppets, props and sets from its shows. Small enough for short attention spans.

  • Where: Rua de Belomonte 61, Porto

O Grande Museu das Casinhas de Bonecas

A museum of dolls' houses, all miniature rooms and tiny furniture. A quiet hour between busier sights.

  • Where: Rua do Alferes Malheiro 128, 4000-057 Porto
  • When: daily, 9:30 to 18:00

One last tip: for SEA LIFE and World of Discoveries, buy tickets online the day before in school holidays. You skip the queue, which matters when you have small children with you.
