Baris here. Porto goes out late. Bars are fairly quiet before 23:00, the streets around Galerias de Paris fill up around midnight, and clubs get going after 1:00. This guide covers where to hear fado, where to drink, where to dance and where to catch live music, with the details I could check.

Where the Night Happens

  • Galerias de Paris and Cândido dos Reis: The busiest bar streets in the city. On Friday and Saturday nights people drink in the street between dozens of bars.
  • Ribeira: Calmer, with terraces and wine bars on the river facing Gaia and the Dom Luís I Bridge.
  • Baixa: The wider downtown, with cocktail bars, tascas and most of the clubs within walking distance of each other.
  • Cedofeita: More local and laid-back, with small bars and live music spots around the gallery district.

Fado in Porto

Fado is Portugal's traditional song, slow and sad, and it is best heard in a small room. In Porto you can go to a short concert with a glass of Port, or make a night of it with dinner. Most places are small, so book.

Concerts

  • Ideal Clube de Fado (Rua do Ateneu Comercial do Porto 32, 4000-380): Traditional fado only. Concerts every day, about an hour long, with two singers and two guitarists. Tickets cost €21 (€16 for ages 6 to 17 and students) and include a glass of Port. Book on fado.club.
  • Casa da Guitarra (Avenida Vímara Peres 49, near the Sé): A shop for traditional Portuguese guitars that holds a 60-minute fado show every day, with a glass of Port at the interval. From Monday to Saturday it also runs "Fado Vitória" at the Mosteiro de São Bento da Vitória, with a self-guided visit of the monastery. Tickets on portofado.com.
  • A Casa do Fado (Igreja de São João Novo, Largo de São João Novo 2, 4050-066): Concerts in the historic centre with a short talk on the origins of fado and a glass of Port. See acasadofado.pt for the current venues.
  • Fado Maior do Porto (Miragaia): Live every night in an old tavern near the river, with two groups, one for Lisbon fado and one for Coimbra fado. No microphones. See fadomaior.pt.

Dinner with Fado

  • Casa da Mariquinhas (Rua de São Sebastião 25, 4050-568, near the Sé): A fado house since 1968. The show comes with dinner, on a minimum spend of starter, main and dessert. Book on casadamariquinhas.pt.
  • Mal Cozinhado (Rua do Outeirinho 13, 4050-450): A traditional Portuguese restaurant with live fado, near the Ribeira. Children under 6 are not admitted. See malcozinhado.com.
  • Taberna Real do Fado (historic centre, near the Cordoaria garden): A fado restaurant named after two old Porto fado houses that no longer exist. Booking recommended.

Fado Vadio

  • Adega Rio Douro (Rua do Ouro 223, 4150-553): A riverside tasca with fado vadio, where amateurs and regulars take turns singing, every Tuesday afternoon from 16:00 to 19:30.

Bars: Wine, Cocktails and Beer

Wine Bars and Tascas

  • Aduela (Rua das Oliveiras 36, 4050-448): A tasca-bar on a small square, with wine, petiscos and tables outside. Open late every day and usually packed.
  • Café Candelabro (Rua da Conceição 3, 4050-215): A bar in a former second-hand bookshop from 1952, with old books still on the shelves. Good for a first drink.
  • Capela Incomum (Travessa do Carregal 77, 4050-167): A wine bar inside a former chapel.
  • Barbarita Tapas Bar (Rua de Santo Ildefonso 366A): Portuguese petiscos, wine and cocktails.

Cocktail Bars

  • Torto (Rua de José Falcão 199): An award-winning cocktail bar in Baixa, with a short menu of signature drinks. Opens at 17:30 from Thursday to Sunday and at 19:00 from Monday to Wednesday. See torto-porto.com.
  • The Royal Cocktail Club (Rua da Fábrica 105, 4050-247): A dim, serious cocktail bar. Open from 19:00, until 2:00 Sunday to Thursday and 3:00 on Friday and Saturday.
  • Apotecário: A speakeasy hidden under the restaurant Curioso, run by bartender Pedro Duarte. There is no fixed menu; the drink is made around your mood.

Beer and Open-Air

  • Letraria (Rua dos Mártires da Liberdade 108, 4000-483): The Porto bar of the Letra craft brewery, with a garden at the back.
  • Base Porto (Passeio dos Clérigos, next to the Clérigos Tower): An open-air garden bar on the lawn above the Passeio dos Clérigos, best in the late afternoon.
  • Galeria de Paris (Rua da Galeria de Paris 56, 4050-284): A bar-restaurant on the street itself, with live music on Thursday and Saturday from 19:00.

Drinks with a View

  • Bar da Ponte Pênsil (Ribeira): A small bar built into one of the surviving pillars of the old suspension bridge, right next to the Dom Luís I Bridge. Live music every day, according to the bar.
  • 17º Restaurante & Bar (top floor of Hotel Dom Henrique Downtown): A restaurant and bar with a 360-degree view over the city.
  • Douro Sky Lounge (rooftop of Hotel Vincci Porto, by the river): Cocktails and food on the roof, daily from 13:00 to 19:00, weather permitting.
  • Ontop (top floor of HF Ipanema Park): A rooftop bar with a pool and views over the river.
  • Espaço Porto Cruz (Vila Nova de Gaia): The Porto Cruz wine space on the Gaia riverfront, with a 360-degree rooftop bar.
  • Rooftop Santa Catarina (on top of the La Vie Porto Baixa shopping centre, Rua de Santa Catarina): A rooftop bar over the downtown roofs. Check its site that it is open before you go.

Clubs

Clubs fill up after 1:00. Dress codes are relaxed almost everywhere.

  • Plano B (Rua de Cândido dos Reis 30, 4050-151): A Porto club for about 20 years, with two rooms for DJ sets (house and techno) and live acts.
  • Maus Hábitos (Rua de Passos Manuel 178, 4th floor): A cultural space on the top floor of a car park building, with a restaurant, exhibitions, concerts and club nights. Programme on maushabitos.com.
  • Gare Porto (Rua da Madeira 182): The city's techno club, near São Bento, with national and international DJs. Usually Friday and Saturday from 23:00 to 6:00; check events on Resident Advisor.
  • Tendinha dos Clérigos (Rua do Conde de Vizela 80, 4050-639): A small rock bar with rock, punk and indie DJ sets at weekends. Opening nights vary, so check their social pages.
  • Hard Club (Mercado Ferreira Borges, near the Palácio da Bolsa): Concert rooms and a restaurant in the 19th-century iron market building. Agenda on hardclubporto.com.

Live Music Beyond Fado

  • Casa da Música (Avenida da Boavista 604-610): Rem Koolhaas's concert hall, with classical, jazz, pop and electronic programmes. Daily guided tours of the building cost €12 (13 to 18: €5; under 12 free with an adult). See casadamusica.com.
  • Hot Five Jazz & Blues Club (Largo Actor Dias 51): Porto's longest-running live music club, open since 2005. Open Thursday to Sunday, 21:30 to 2:30; no entry under 16. Shows on hotfive.pt.
  • Coliseu Porto (Rua de Passos Manuel 137): The historic theatre for big concerts, ballet and shows. Agenda on coliseu.pt.
  • Super Bock Arena - Pavilhão Rosa Mota (Jardins do Palácio de Cristal): The arena for large international tours. Popular shows sell out early.
  • RUA Tapas & Music Bar (Travessa de Cedofeita 24, 4050-448): Tapas and live music every night.
  • M.Ou.Co. (Rua de Frei Heitor Pinto 65, Bonfim): A music-themed hotel that also runs a regular concert programme. Listings on Agenda Porto.

Porto is generally safe at night, but watch your phone and wallet in the crowds on Galerias de Paris. Taxis and ride-hailing apps are easy to find when you want to go home.
