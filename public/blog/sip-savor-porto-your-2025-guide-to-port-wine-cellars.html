Hello, Porto lovers, Baris here. Port wine is made in the Douro Valley, but it is aged, blended and bottled in the lodges of Vila Nova de Gaia, right across the river from Ribeira. Visiting one or two of them is the easiest way to understand what you're drinking. This guide covers the lodges I'd send a friend to, what each visit includes, and a few wine bars on the Porto side for the evening.

The Lodges: Across the Bridge in Vila Nova de Gaia

Walk over the lower deck of the Dom Luís I Bridge and you're in Gaia, with the lodge names painted on the rooftops along the waterfront. The wine comes down from the Douro and rests here in cool cellars, in casks and big wooden vats. Most lodges want a booking for the guided visit, and in summer the popular slots go days ahead, so book online before you cross.

Cálem: Museum, Tasting and Fado

Address: Av. de Diogo Leite 344, 4400-111 Vila Nova de Gaia

Cálem is the first lodge you reach on the riverfront. The visit starts in an interactive museum about the Douro and how Port is made, then goes into the cellars with a guide and ends with a tasting. The other option is Tour, Taste & Fado, which adds a live fado show in the cellars. It's a good first visit if you know nothing about Port.

  • Visits: Tour & Taste, or Tour, Taste & Fado
  • Prices and times: check tour.calem.pt before you go

Graham's 1890 Lodge: The View From the Hill

Address: Rua do Agro 141, 4400-003 Vila Nova de Gaia (on foot: Rua Rei Ramiro 514)

Graham's has been in this lodge since 1890, and all its wine is aged here. It sits high above the river, so it's a steep walk up, and the view back over Porto is the reward. Every visit is guided by one of their staff and ends with a tasting; you can also pay for food pairings or a private tasting in the Boardroom. If you don't want the tour, you can just sit and order Ports by the glass in the tasting room, no booking needed.

  • Visits: November to March 10:00 to 17:00, April to October 10:00 to 17:30 (booking required)
  • Tastings by the glass: November to March 11:00 to 17:30, April to October 10:00 to 17:30
  • Prices: see grahams-port.com

Taylor's: An Audio Tour at Your Own Pace

Address: Rua do Choupelo 250, 4400-088 Vila Nova de Gaia

Taylor's was founded in 1692, and its cellars are about 300 years old. The visit is self-guided: you scan a QR code and follow an audio guide on your phone (13 languages, bring headphones), with films and exhibits along the way. It ends with three wines: Chip Dry white Port, a Late Bottled Vintage and a 10 Year Old Tawny. Older Tawnies and Vintage Ports are available in the tasting room if you want to go further.

  • Cellar: every day, 10:00 to 18:15
  • Tasting room: every day, 10:00 to 19:30
  • Prices: book on taylor.pt

Cockburn's: Barrels and Coopers

Address: Rua Serpa Pinto 346, 4400-307 Vila Nova de Gaia (by car: Rua Dona Leonor de Freitas 225)

Cockburn's says it has the largest wooden Port cellar in Gaia, and it still has its own team of coopers, who repair the oak casks on site. They work Monday to Friday, so go on a weekday if you want to see them. The guided visit with a tasting takes about an hour and a half, and you choose the tasting: a Tawny flight, a premium tasting with chocolate, or a bigger one with cheese and a custard tart. Visits are by reservation only.

  • Visits: November to March 10:00 to 17:00, April to October 10:00 to 17:30
  • Closed: 23, 24 and 25 December and 1 January
  • Prices: see cockburns.com

Sandeman: The Don

Address: Largo Miguel Bombarda 47, 4400-222 Vila Nova de Gaia

You'll know Sandeman by the caped figure of the Don on the riverfront. Sandeman wine has aged in this building since 1811, and on the standard visit "the Don" leads you through the vats and casks. Of the lodges here, Sandeman is the one that lists its prices on its website.

  • Porto Sandeman Visit: €23, 50 minutes, three wines (White, Ruby and Tawny)
  • Port and chocolate: €32, one hour, three wines with chocolate truffles
  • Old Tawnies Visit: €53, 90 minutes, the 10, 20, 30 and 40 Year Old Tawnies
  • Book: sandeman.com

Back in Porto: Wine Bars

After a lodge visit, cross back over the bridge. These bars pour Port and Portuguese table wine by the glass, and they're where I'd go for a second round.

Wine Quay Bar

Address: Muro dos Bacalhoeiros 111/112, 4050-080 Porto

A small bar on the old quay wall in Ribeira, looking straight across at the Gaia lodges. It opened about 14 years ago and calls itself the first wine bar in the city. Portuguese wines and tapas boards. The outside tables go fast at sunset.

  • When: 16:00 to 23:00 (per Visit Porto)

Prova

Address: Rua Ferreira Borges 86, 4050-252 Porto

Diogo Amado's wine bar between Largo de São Domingos and the Hard Club. Tell the staff what you like and they'll pour you something from a smaller producer. It stays open late.

  • When: 17:00 to 01:00 (per prova.com.pt)

A Cave do Bon Vivant

Address: Rua de Santa Catarina 763, 4000-454 Porto

Natural, organic and biodynamic wine, no printed list, and one price for any wine by the glass. They let you taste before they pour. The food is simple and seasonal, with produce from Bolhão and from their own small farm in Trofa.

  • When: Monday from 17:00, Tuesday to Saturday from the afternoon, until midnight; check acavedobonvivant.pt

Portologia

Address: Rua de São João 28-30, 4050-522 Porto

A Port specialist a short walk up from Ribeira. The focus is on small producers, with themed tasting flights, and you can buy bottles to take home.

Vinum at Graham's

Address: Rua do Agro 141, 4400-003 Vila Nova de Gaia

The restaurant next to Graham's lodge, with the same view over the river. The menu draws on the Douro, Trás-os-Montes, the Minho and the Atlantic, and you can book it together with a lodge visit and tasting.

My last tip: do the lodge in the afternoon and the wine bar in the evening, and book the lodge first. Saúde!
