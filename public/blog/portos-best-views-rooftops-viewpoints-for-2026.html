Porto is built on steep hills on both sides of the Douro, so you're never far from a view. This guide covers the public viewpoints first, most of them free, and then the rooftop bars on both sides of the river for when you want a drink with it.

The Classic Viewpoints

These are the miradouros that give you the postcard shots. Most cost nothing, and the best time for all of them is the hour before sunset.

Miradouro da Serra do Pilar

Ask a local for the best view of Porto and most will point across the river to Gaia. The terrace in front of the Serra do Pilar monastery looks straight at the upper deck of the Dom Luís I Bridge and the Ribeira climbing the hill behind it. The monastery is part of a UNESCO World Heritage site. Walk over the bridge's upper deck to get there; the view on the way is half the point.

  • Where: Largo de Avis, 4430-329 Vila Nova de Gaia
  • Price: The terrace is free. Inside, the monastery and its exhibition cost €2, or €4 including the climb to the dome.
  • When (monastery): Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:30 from April to October, and to 17:30 from November to March. See culturaportugal.gov.pt

Jardim do Morro

Just below Serra do Pilar, at the Gaia end of the bridge, this sloping garden fills up on sunny evenings with locals and visitors waiting for the sunset. There are often street musicians. Get there an hour early on weekends if you want a decent patch of grass; plenty of people bring their own drinks and snacks.

  • Where: Avenida da República, 4430-148 Vila Nova de Gaia
  • Price: Free

Miradouro da Vitória

Back on the Porto side, near the old Jewish quarter, this viewpoint is on private land that the owners leave open to the public. You see the Sé, the bridge and the port lodges in Gaia without much in the way. It's not landscaped and it's poorly lit at night, so watch your footing. To find it, wander the streets behind the São Bento da Vitória monastery.

  • Where: Rua de São Bento da Vitória 11, Porto
  • Price: Free

Torre dos Clérigos

For a 360-degree view from the middle of the city, climb the baroque Clérigos Tower. The stairs are narrow and there are a lot of them, but from the top you can see the whole historic centre, the river and, on a clear day, the Atlantic. It's a good way to get your bearings on the first day.

  • Where: Rua de São Filipe de Nery, 4050-546 Porto
  • When: Daily, 9:00 to 19:00 (last entry 30 minutes before closing). Night visits to the tower, 19:00 to 23:00, only at Easter, in summer and at Christmas.
  • Price: €10 for the tower and museum during the day, €7 students; night tower ticket €5. Book a time slot at torredosclerigos.pt to avoid the queue.

Rooftop Bars

When you want to sit down and have a drink with the view. Opening times for terraces depend on the weather and the season, so check before you go.

On the Gaia Side

Looking back across the river from Gaia gives you the full front of the Ribeira, which is why so many of the best terraces are here.

Terrace Lounge 360º
On the top floor of Espaço Porto Cruz, the Porto Cruz port house on the Gaia riverfront. The view goes all the way round, and the drinks list leans on port cocktails, so this is the place to try a port and tonic. It gets busy around sunset.
Where: Largo Miguel Bombarda 23, 4400-222 Vila Nova de Gaia. Hours and events: espacoportocruz.pt

Dick's Bar & Bistro at The Yeatman
The bar of The Yeatman, a five-star wine hotel high on the Gaia hillside. The terrace looks across the Douro to the old town and the wine list is long. It's expensive and fairly formal. If you're not staying at the hotel, contact them before you go.
Where: Rua do Choupelo, 4400-088 Vila Nova de Gaia. See the-yeatman-hotel.com

Vinum at Graham's
A restaurant and wine bar inside Graham's port lodge, with a terrace over the river and views of the old barrel cellar inside. The cooking mixes Portuguese and Basque dishes. Book ahead.
Where: Rua do Agro 141, 4400-003 Vila Nova de Gaia. Open every day, 12:00 to midnight. Bookings via grahams-port.com

On the Porto Side

The Porto terraces are smaller and more tucked away, with different angles on the river and the rooftops.

Mirajazz
A small rooftop at the top of the Escadas do Caminho Novo in Miragaia, open since 2017. It looks over the river towards the Arrábida Bridge and the sea, serves Portuguese wines, and has live jazz on its programme. It's small, so book or arrive early.
Where: Escadas do Caminho Novo 11, 4050-554 Porto. Programme and bookings: mirajazz.pt

17º Restaurante & Bar
On the 17th floor of the Hotel Dom Henrique Downtown, one of the highest points in the centre. There's a lift from the hotel lobby. It's a smart place for dinner or a quiet cocktail, and the bar stays open late.
Where: Rua do Bolhão 223, 4000-112 Porto. Bar every day 12:00 to 02:00; restaurant 12:30 to 15:00 and from 19:00. See decimosetimo.pt

Douro Sky Lounge
The rooftop lounge of the Vincci Porto hotel, in the old fish market building on the riverfront at Massarelos. It looks over the river and the Arrábida Bridge towards the mouth of the Douro, a bit away from the historic centre.
Where: Alameda de Basílio Teles 29, 4150-127 Porto. See vincciporto.com

My last tip: don't try to do them all in one day. Pick one viewpoint in the morning to get your bearings and one terrace for the sunset.
