YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Pride, Poets & Post-Rock

September 10-16, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

As the first whispers of autumn arrive, Porto answers with a burst of life and colour. This weekend, the city is a vibrant stage for celebration, from the joyous energy of Porto Pride to the soul-stirring orchestral sounds of Sigur Rós. It's a week for every mood: wander through the pages at the Book Fair, journey back in time at a medieval festival, and find a rhythm that speaks to you.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Explore the Art District

For a dose of contemporary culture, spend an afternoon wandering down Rua de Miguel Bombarda. Known as Porto's official arts district, this street and its surrounding lanes are packed with independent galleries showcasing emerging and established artists. Every couple of months they host simultaneous openings, creating a buzzing, festive atmosphere perfect for gallery hopping and discovering your next favorite artist.
