YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Symphonies & Senses

April 30 - May 6, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto strikes a beautiful chord between exhilarating energy and refined culture. From the thundering riffs of a major metal festival to the soulful strings of a classical orchestra and the immersive glow of new art exhibitions, the city invites you to indulge every one of your senses. Come discover the vibrant harmony of it all.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Book Ahead for The Big Events

With major concerts like the Gipsy Kings, popular venues like Casa da Música, and world-famous attractions like Livraria Lello on the agenda, this is a busy week in Porto. To avoid disappointment, book your tickets online and in advance wherever possible. This not only secures your spot but often saves you from waiting in long queues, giving you more time to enjoy the city.
