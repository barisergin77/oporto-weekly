Tip of the Week

Carry Cash for Markets: When visiting Porto's vibrant food markets like Mercado do Bolhão, it's always a good idea to have some cash on hand. While larger vendors might accept cards, many smaller, traditional stalls prefer or only accept cash for their fresh produce and unique artisanal products.
