YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Symphony & Stories

September 3-9, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto’s heart beats to a grand rhythm. Avenida dos Aliados transforms into an open-air stage for not one, but two magnificent orchestral concerts, filling the city with the sound of opera and street symphonies. As we bid farewell to the beloved Book Fair, a new energy arrives with vibrant percussion parades and the thrilling launch of an international performing arts festival. It's a week of powerful finales and exciting new beginnings.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Enjoying the Aliados Concerts

With two major free concerts transforming Avenida dos Aliados this weekend, the key is to arrive early to secure a good spot with a clear view of the stage. Consider bringing a small blanket to sit on the pavement, or grab a drink at one of the surrounding café terraces. Embrace the communal atmosphere—it’s a uniquely Porto experience to share world-class music with thousands of others in the heart of the city.
