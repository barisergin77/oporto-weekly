YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

A City in Bloom

May 14-20, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto truly comes alive. The air is buzzing with the start of festival season as the beloved Festas do Senhor de Matosinhos kicks off, while the city’s stages are graced by the powerful performances of the FITEI theatre festival. From stadium roars to museum whispers, it’s a week to dive headfirst into the vibrant culture of our city.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating the Festas do Senhor de Matosinhos

As the massive Festas do Senhor de Matosinhos begins, be prepared for crowds, especially during weekend evenings. For the best experience, consider taking the metro to Matosinhos Sul to avoid traffic. Go with an empty stomach—the festival is famous for its food stalls selling everything from farturas (churros) to grilled meats. Check the official schedule for major concerts, but don't be afraid to just wander and soak in the vibrant, traditional atmosphere.
