Festive Sparks and Farewells

November 26 - December 2, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

Welcome to the final days of November. Porto officially kicks off the holiday season this weekend with the Aliados lights and an outdoor concert. It is also a week of notable goodbyes, as we catch the final stage performance from PAUS at Casa da Música. Bundle up and enjoy the crisp evenings.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating the Aliados Lights

When heading down to Aliados for the Christmas lights inauguration this Saturday, leave the car behind. Surrounding streets like Rua de Ceuta and Rua do Almada become completely gridlocked. Take the Metro to Trindade and walk down, or catch the yellow line straight to Aliados. If you are meeting friends, pick a spot away from the main tree to find each other easily in the crowd.
