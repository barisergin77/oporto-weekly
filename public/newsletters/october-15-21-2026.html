YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Strings, Sips & Samba

October 15-21, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto pulses with a vibrant rhythm. The crisp autumn air is filled with the sounds of Brazilian samba, iconic Portuguese pop-rock, and cinematic scores as cultural festivals take center stage. From a city-wide race that gets the heart pumping to intimate film screenings and thrilling concerts, it's a week to dive headfirst into the rich tapestry of experiences our city so generously offers.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Master the Porto Night Out

Porto's nightlife famously starts late. For a perfect evening, embrace the local rhythm: begin around 9 PM with a relaxed dinner or an intimate Fado show in Ribeira or Foz. Afterwards, head to the Baixa district. The streets around Galerias de Paris only truly come alive after midnight, so grab a drink at a smaller bar like Aduela or Bonaparte Downtown before diving into the main clubs like Plano B or Maus Hábitos, which often stay open until dawn.
