YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Summer's Opening Note

June 4-10, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

As the city catches its breath between the whirlwind of Serralves em Festa and the anticipation of NOS Primavera Sound, this week offers a moment of pure magic. It’s a time for intimate discoveries, from candlelit concerts in a palace to the return of the beloved historic train journeying through the Douro Valley. Let’s savour this perfect interlude, filled with music, art, and the first true whispers of summer.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Get Ready for São João

While Porto's biggest street party, the Festa de São João, culminates on the night of June 23rd, the city's festive spirit is already beginning to sparkle. Keep an eye out for the first signs of the celebration: pots of sweet basil (*manjericos*) appearing in shops and the smell of grilled sardines in the air. From June 8th, you can even visit the traditional São João Community Waterfall display at Mercado do Bolhão to get a taste of the magic to come.
