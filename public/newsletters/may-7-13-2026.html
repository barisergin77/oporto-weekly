YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Academic Spirit, City Rhythms

May 7-13, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto is electric with the energy of its students as the Queima das Fitas fills the streets with parades and tradition. Beyond the academic fervor, our city's stages come alive with everything from soulful indie folk to vibrant Brazilian beats. It’s a week to embrace the youthful spirit, discover new sounds, and celebrate the unique culture that defines Porto.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating the Academic Procession

The Queima das Fitas' Cortejo Académico is a highlight of the year, but it significantly impacts city traffic. To best enjoy it, leave the car behind. Find a spot along the Rua dos Clérigos or Avenida dos Aliados early to get a great view of the floats. Embrace the chaos, enjoy the student chants, and be prepared for some street closures. It's a fantastic, free spectacle of Porto's vibrant academic culture!
