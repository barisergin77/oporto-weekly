YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

An Autumn Artscape

September 24-30, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto truly comes alive with creativity. The city welcomes the inaugural ARTePorto, a landmark art fair decades in the making, transforming the historic Alfândega into a haven for collectors and connoisseurs. Beyond the canvases, the beloved Serralves Autumn Festival fills the park with family fun, while a vibrant soundtrack of candlelight concerts and indie festivals provides the perfect score for an unforgettable autumn weekend.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Conquering the Weekend Market Scene

Saturday is the undisputed king of Porto's market days, with traditional spots like Mercado do Bolhão and the organic market in Parque da Cidade in full swing. But don't despair if you're a Sunday visitor! While many classics rest, modern food halls like Mercado Bom Sucesso and the Time Out Market are buzzing. They offer a fantastic alternative, bringing some of the city's best chefs and diverse culinary stalls together under one roof for a perfect weekend meal.
