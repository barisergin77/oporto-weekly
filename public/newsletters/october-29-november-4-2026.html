YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

City of Spectres & Song

October 29 - November 4, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto embraces the magic of autumn and the thrill of Halloween. The city comes alive with everything from haunting gothic metal concerts and spirited parties to soulful Fado in ancient monasteries and poignant choir performances for All Saints' Day. It's a week to celebrate, reflect, and discover the many songs our city has to sing.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Discover Porto's Spooky Side

Beyond the Halloween parties, embrace the city's atmospheric charm with an evening stroll. Wander the narrow lanes of Ribeira and Miragaia after dusk, where ancient stone walls and dimly lit alleys whisper tales of the past. For a truly memorable experience, visit the catacombs beneath the São Francisco Church—a hauntingly beautiful reminder of Porto's long and layered history.
