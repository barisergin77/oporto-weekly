YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Symphony & Sunshine

July 30 - August 5, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto is a city of beautiful contrasts. The powerful chords of a symphonic rock tribute and a legendary indie artist fill our grandest venues, while the delicate notes of classical piano drift from Casa da Música. Art also takes centre stage with new exhibitions pushing creative boundaries. It's the perfect time to soak in the culture, and perhaps, catch a traditional puppet show by the sea as the sun sets. Enjoy the harmony.

Tip of the Week

Build the Perfect Park Picnic

Make the most of the summer weather with a classic Porto weekend. On Saturday morning, head to the Feira de Produtores Biológicos in Parque da Cidade. Fill your basket with fresh organic fruit, artisanal bread, local cheese, and honey. Once you're done, you're already in the perfect spot: find a shady tree in the sprawling park, lay down a blanket, and enjoy the city's freshest al fresco lunch.
