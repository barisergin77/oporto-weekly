YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Autumn's Overture

September 17-23, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto feels like a city in full celebration of its own creative spirit. Two beloved institutions—the historic Mercado do Bolhão and the vibrant Miguel Bombarda art district—are marking major anniversaries, filling the streets with special events. Add a major marathon along the Douro, a headline concert from a Brazilian music icon, and a wave of new exhibitions, and you have a week brimming with energy. It's the perfect moment to dive in and experience the city at its best.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating the Half Marathon

On Sunday morning, the city's heart beats to the rhythm of the Porto Half Marathon. While it's an inspiring spectacle, be aware of widespread road closures, especially along the Douro riverfront in both Porto and Gaia. Plan to use the Metro for travel, as many bus routes will be diverted. If you're near the route, find a spot early, grab a coffee, and cheer on the thousands of runners!
