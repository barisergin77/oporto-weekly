YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Porto's Summer Stage

July 16-22, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

Porto is absolutely electric this week, with the city transforming into one giant stage. The legendary MEO Marés Vivas festival brings world-class music to the coast, while the city's art scene explodes with the incredible Circuitos'26, a sprawling celebration of contemporary art. Whether you're dancing on the beach, gallery-hopping through Cedofeita, or treating the little ones to the magic of the Panda Festival, there's a rhythm for everyone to find.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating the City of Art

With over 60 venues participating in the Circuitos'26 art festival, it can feel wonderfully overwhelming. Our tip? Don't try to see it all. Instead, pick a neighbourhood—like the artsy Bombarda district or the historic center—and spend an afternoon exploring its galleries and pop-up events. Grab a festival map and let yourself wander; the real magic is in the unexpected discoveries you'll make between the main stops.
