YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Autumn's Creative Pulse

October 1-7, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

Welcome to the first week of October! Porto ushers in the new season with the city-wide 'Outono em Festa', a month-long celebration of autumn's charms. This week is a cultural feast, offering everything from a symphony of retro video game music at Casa da Música to the launch of a major contemporary art exhibition. Let's dive in.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Take an Art Walk Down Rua Miguel Bombarda

With the Porto Contemporary Art Exhibition kicking off this week, there's no better time to explore the city's official "Art District" on Rua Miguel Bombarda. This street is packed with a high concentration of independent contemporary art galleries. Even without a specific plan, a simple stroll down the street will lead you to surprising discoveries, from emerging local artists to established international names. Pop into a few galleries, grab a coffee at a nearby cafe, and soak in the creative atmosphere.
