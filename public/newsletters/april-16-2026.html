TIP OF THE WEEK

Embrace the Market Magic

For an authentic taste of Porto, don't miss the bustling food markets this weekend! From the historic Mercado do Bolhão to the modern Mercado do Bom Sucesso, each offers a unique culinary adventure. Arrive early for the freshest produce, and be sure to sample local delicacies. It's not just shopping; it's a vibrant slice of Porto life.
