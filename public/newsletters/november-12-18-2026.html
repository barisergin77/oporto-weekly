Amazonia to Ballet

November 12-18, 2026
From the Editor

November brings a sudden chill to the city, but the cultural calendar is packed. This week features international ballet at the Coliseu, classic Chaplin silent films downtown, and Casa da Música's tribute to the Amazon. Grab a thick coat and head out.

Tip of the Week

Wrap up for the evening chill

November evenings in Porto get damp and cold very quickly once the sun goes down around 17:15. If you are heading to a gig at Hard Club or dinner in Ribeira, pack a solid windbreaker or wool coat. The breeze off the Douro river cuts right through light autumn jackets.
