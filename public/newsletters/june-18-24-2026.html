YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

São João's Spark

June 18-24, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

Welcome to a Porto buzzing with anticipation. You can feel it in the air—the scent of grilling sardines, the colourful streamers crisscrossing the streets. This is the week the city truly comes alive, building towards its biggest party, São João. From grand symphonies to craft beer festivals, every event is touched by this infectious, festive spirit. Let’s dive in.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Embrace the Hammer!

As you wander Porto this week, you'll see street vendors selling colourful plastic hammers. This is essential São João equipment! The tradition is to playfully bop friends and strangers on the head as a gesture of good luck and camaraderie. Don't be shy—buy a hammer, join in the fun, and don't be alarmed if a smiling local gives you a friendly bonk. It’s all part of the magic that makes this festival so unique.
