YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Films and Fugues

November 19-25, 2026
FROM THE EDITOR

Autumn in Porto brings a distinct cultural rhythm. This week, the Porto/Post/Doc festival takes over Batalha, while Serralves hosts contemporary dance and Casa da Música shakes the dust off its pipe organ. Grab a coat and head out to the city's indoor stages.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating Batalha during Porto/Post/Doc

With Porto/Post/Doc taking over Batalha Centro de Cinema this week, the area gets busy. If you plan to catch multiple screenings, grab your tickets early online, as the evening sessions sell out fast. Between films, duck into Café Célia on Rua de Entreparedes for a quick coffee and a warm pastel de nata, far from the main square crowds.
