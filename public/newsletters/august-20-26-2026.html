YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Summer's Grand Crescendo

August 20-26, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto feels like a grand celebration of summer's final act. The air is electric with music from legendary festivals, the thrill of world-class athletics by the sea, and the quiet discovery of newly opened historic doors. It's a time for both big moments and intimate creative pursuits—a perfect blend for saying farewell to August.

TIP OF THE WEEK

How to Find *All* the Events

While Porto's official tourism site is great for weekend planning, it doesn't always have a comprehensive daily list. For the most up-to-the-minute details on everything happening—from small gallery openings to last-minute concerts—be sure to check local news and event sites like `cm-porto.pt` (the city council's agenda) and `portoalive.pt`. They provide a deeper, more granular look at the city's vibrant cultural pulse.
