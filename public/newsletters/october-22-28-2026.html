TIP OF THE WEEK

Embrace the Street Party

Porto’s nightlife starts late and often happens right on the street. For a truly local experience, head to the Galerias de Paris district after 11 PM. Don’t feel pressured to commit to one bar; the real party is on the cobblestones. Grab an affordable drink from one of the many small storefronts and join the crowd outside. It’s a wonderfully social and fluid way to spend an evening, hopping from one group of friends to another under the city lights.
