YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Academic Spirit Soars

May 1-7, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto truly comes alive with the vibrant energy of Queima das Fitas, a spectacular academic festival that takes over the city. Beyond the student celebrations, we've curated an exciting lineup of live music, captivating art openings, delicious food experiences, and family-friendly activities, ensuring there's something special for everyone to enjoy.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating Queima das Fitas Festivities

With Queima das Fitas in full swing, expect larger crowds, especially in the city center and around the Queimódromo. Plan your transport using Porto's excellent public transport network (metro, bus) which will be reinforced. Keep an eye on your belongings, stay hydrated, and embrace the joyous, festive spirit responsibly.
