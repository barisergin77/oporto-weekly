YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Porto's Autumn Stage

October 8-14, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

Porto is putting on a show this week, dear reader. Autumn's arrival brings with it a cultural tapestry woven from diverse threads. We see the curtain rise on two major festivals—the whimsical International Puppet Theatre Festival and the soulful Blues Juke Joint Weekend. Meanwhile, a beloved cult tribute band takes its final bow at the Coliseu. It's a week of enchanting beginnings and memorable farewells, inviting us to explore every corner of the city's vibrant stage.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating a Festival Weekend

With both the International Puppet Festival (FIMP) and the Blues Juke Joint Weekend running simultaneously, this weekend is a cultural feast. To make the most of it, check their official programs online beforehand. Events are spread across multiple venues, and some performances or workshops may require advance booking. A little planning will ensure you don't miss the shows you're most excited to see!
