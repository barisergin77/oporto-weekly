YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Saints, Cycles, & Song

August 13-19, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto offers a beautiful blend of sacred tradition and thrilling competition. Feel the city's deep-rooted faith at the Festa de Nossa Senhora da Assunção, then cheer on the athletes at the exhilarating finale of the Volta a Portugal. It’s a week to experience the full spectrum of Porto's vibrant soul.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating the Public Holiday

Remember that this Saturday, August 15th, is Assumption Day, a public holiday in Portugal. While many shops and some attractions might have reduced hours or be closed, it’s the perfect day to embrace local culture. Head to the Porto Cathedral to experience the Festa de Nossa Senhora da Assunção, a truly authentic celebration that will be the highlight of the day. Public transport will run on a holiday schedule, so plan your travels accordingly.
