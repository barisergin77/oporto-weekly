Tip of the Week

Navigating Porto's Nightlife: Public transport in Porto usually stops around 1 AM. If you're planning a late night out, especially on Thursdays, Fridays, or Saturdays, remember to budget for ride-sharing services or consider walking in groups to get back to your accommodation safely.
