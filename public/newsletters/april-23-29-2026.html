YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Revolution in the Air

April 23-29, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto is electric with the spirit of freedom as we celebrate the 52nd anniversary of the Carnation Revolution. Join the city for concerts and fireworks on the 25th, but don't miss the other cultural blossoms—from the passionate rhythms of an international tango festival to the savory delights of the much-anticipated Francesinha Festival. It’s a week to celebrate, indulge, and explore.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating the 25th of April

The celebrations for Freedom Day on Friday will bring large crowds, especially around Avenida dos Aliados. For the best view of the concert and fireworks, arrive early. Public transport will be the best way to get around, as many streets will be closed. On Saturday, the family events are a more relaxed way to soak up the atmosphere. It's a day of national pride, so embrace the spirit, grab a fresh fino, and enjoy the festivities!
