YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Fables & Fanfare

August 27 - September 2, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

As August draws to a close, Porto offers a final flourish of summer magic. This week is truly special, anchored by one of the city's most unique traditions—the whimsical Paper Costume Parade marching through Foz. It’s also a week of final chances, with major art exhibitions taking their last bow. From vibrant electronic beats in the park to quiet poetry readings, it’s a perfect week to capture the diverse spirit of the city before September arrives.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating the Paper Parade

For the best view of the incredible paper costumes at the São Bartolomeu parade this Sunday, find a spot early along Passeio Alegre. While the parade ends at Praia do Ourigo, the riverside promenade offers a more spacious and picturesque vantage point. After the procession, join the locals for the traditional 'holy bath' in the sea—a refreshing and truly authentic Porto experience.
