YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

A Festive Glow

June 25 - July 1, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

Porto is still buzzing with the infectious energy of São João, and while the main night has passed, the celebrations are far from over. This week, the city's festive spirit continues with vibrant street parades and traditional parties, inviting you to join in the final echoes of Portugal's most beloved festival. Beyond the party, discover a rich tapestry of art, music, and stories waiting for you in every corner.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Explore the Art Block

For a different side of Porto's culture, take an afternoon to wander down Rua de Miguel Bombarda. This street is the heart of the city's contemporary art scene, packed with independent galleries, concept stores, and artist studios. You never know what you'll discover, and it's the perfect place to find a unique souvenir or simply be inspired by Porto's creative energy.
