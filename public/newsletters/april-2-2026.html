Porto Markets This Weekend

  • Mercado do Bolhão: Sat (8:00-18:00). Historic market for fresh produce and local foods.
  • Mercado do Bom Sucesso: Daily (8:00-23:00). Diverse food stalls and restaurants.
  • Porto Belo Market: Sat (10:00-19:00), Praça de Carlos Alberto. Artistic creations, vintage, organic products.
  • Feira da Vandoma: Sat (8:00-13:00). Traditional flea market for second-hand items.
  • Feira da Productores Bio: Sat (8:30-13:30), Parque da Cidade. Organic produce from local farmers.
  • Time Out Market Porto: Daily (10:00-23:00 on Sundays), São Bento station. Top Porto restaurants.
  • Mercado Beira Rio (Gaia): Daily (10:00-22:00). Food market near Port wine lodges.
  • Feira dos Passarinhos: Sun (7:00-13:00), Alameda das Fontainhas. Unique market for domestic birds.

Taste Porto's Easter Delights

As Easter approaches, make sure to try Folar da Páscoa, a traditional Portuguese Easter bread. It comes in sweet or savory varieties and is a staple in local bakeries this time of year. Perfect for a holiday treat!
