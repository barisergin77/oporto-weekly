YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Porto's Summer Pulse

July 23-29, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto is alive with sound. From the soulful riffs of the Porto Blues Fest in the stunning Palácio de Cristal gardens to the stadium-sized energy of rock star Juanes, there's a rhythm for everyone. Beyond the main stages, discover free community celebrations, nostalgic dance parties, and intimate gigs that capture the true spirit of a Porto summer.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating Weekend Markets

Planning a market visit? Remember that Saturday is king for traditional experiences. The iconic Mercado do Bolhão is a must-see for fresh produce and a vibrant atmosphere, but it's closed on Sundays. For a Sunday food adventure, head to modern food halls like Time Out Market, Mercado Bom Sucesso, or Mercado Beira-Rio in Gaia. They are buzzing all day with incredible food stalls from local chefs and are the perfect place to sample the best of Porto's cuisine.
