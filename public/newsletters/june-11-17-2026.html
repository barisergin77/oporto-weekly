YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

The City in Sound

June 11-17, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This is the week Porto turns up the volume. The city pulses with the electric energy of NOS Primavera Sound, drawing music lovers from around the globe to the stunning Parque da Cidade. But even amidst the festival anthems, you can hear the first festive chords of São João, our beloved city celebration, beginning with traditional folk music in the streets. It's a perfect blend of international rhythm and local heart—let's dive in.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Festival by the Sea

The Primavera Sound festival is set in the sprawling Parque da Cidade, but don't just stick to the stages. The park's unique location means you can take a 10-minute walk west and find yourself on the shores of the Atlantic at Praia do Castelo do Queijo. It's the perfect way to reset between sets, catch a stunning sunset over the ocean, and enjoy a different side of the festival experience entirely.
