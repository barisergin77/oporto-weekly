YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Art All Hours

May 21-27, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

This week, Porto is bursting with creative energy and timeless tradition. The city prepares for the extraordinary 50-hour art marathon of Serralves em Festa, while just next door, the sky over Matosinhos will light up with festival fireworks. Whether you're seeking artistic immersion, live music, or the simple joy of an outdoor market, there’s a memorable experience waiting for you.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Navigating the Weekend Markets

To beat the biggest crowds at the iconic Mercado do Bolhão, aim to arrive before 11 AM on Saturday. For a more relaxed Sunday food experience, head to the modern Mercado Bom Sucesso, which stays open all day and offers a wide variety of food stalls perfect for a leisurely lunch or drink.
