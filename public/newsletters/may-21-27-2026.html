YOUR WEEKLY PORTO GUIDE

Festival Season Begins

May 21-27, 2026

FROM THE EDITOR

Welcome to your weekly guide! As Porto warms into the final days of May, a palpable energy fills the air. This week is a testament to the city's vibrant spirit, headlined by the kick-off of the much-anticipated North Music Festival in the stunning gardens of Serralves. From legendary musical performances and captivating new art exhibitions to the simple joy of discovering a local market, there's a unique rhythm to the city right now. Let's dive in.

TIP OF THE WEEK

Discover Porto's Hidden History by Night

For a truly unique perspective on the city, join a free nighttime guided tour of the Agramonte Cemetery this Saturday. Part of the 16th Cultural Cycle of Porto's Cemeteries, this tour delves into the rich art, architecture, and stories held within one of the city's most iconic monumental spaces. It’s a fascinating and atmospheric way to connect with Porto's past, but remember that advance registration is required to secure your spot.
